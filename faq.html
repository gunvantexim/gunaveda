Safety & Suitability

Moringa and Beetroot are natural whole foods generally safe for children over 2 years. Start with ¼–½ tsp and consult a paediatrician first.
Moringa leaf is traditionally used during lactation in India. However, pregnant women should consult their doctor before using any supplement including Moringa, as very high doses are not recommended during pregnancy.
